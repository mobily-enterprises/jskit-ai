# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Skeleton loaders are pale placeholders again: every `v-skeleton-loader` bone (text, heading, avatar, image, button, chip, divider and ossein) is `surface-container-highest`, Material 3's most emphasized neutral container, in light and dark. They were solid `on-surface`, near black in light and near white in dark: Vuetify paints a bone in on-surface at the theme's `border-opacity`, which material-web sets to 1 so that Material 3's outline roles draw at full strength. Material 3 has no skeleton component; its one placeholder colour, Wear's `PlaceholderDefaults.color` (on-surface at 10% over surface-container), lands next to surface-container-highest in both schemes. Each bone's shimmer still sweeps over it, forced colours keep Vuetify's `canvastext` bones, and no app needs an override of its own.
- Linear progress indicators have Material 3's `secondary-container` track (a `VProgressLinear` default `bgColor`) beside the bar and stop indicator in the app's `color`: Vuetify paints the track in the bar's own colour at the same `border-opacity`, so the track was as solid as the bar, determinate, indeterminate and wavy alike. An app's `bg-color` still sets the track. material-web's browser test measures every coloured bone, its shimmer and each track, light and dark.

- Sliders and range sliders are Material 3's slider: Vuetify's pill variant by default, Material 3's extra-small (default) slider, with a 16dp track, a 4x44dp handle bar set apart from the track by a gap on either side, and a stop indicator at the end of the inactive track; the active track and the handle are `primary`, and the inactive track is `secondary-container` at full strength (the new `trackColor` default). They were Vuetify's default variant, a 4dp track under a round, raised 20dp handle. The md3 blueprint's `color: "primary"` made Vuetify draw the inactive track in `primary` faded to 38% (`v-slider-track__background--opacity`; 24% for the pill variant), and an app's `track-color` was faded the same way, which left a `secondary-container` track nearly invisible; a range slider had no colour at all, so its tracks and handles were a grey `surface-variant`. On a 4dp track, the pale `secondary-container` alone stays hard to see on a light container (1.05:1 on `surface-container-high`), which is why Material 3 draws it 16dp tall and ends it with the stop indicator. A disabled slider is on-surface at 38% for the active track and the handle and 12% for the inactive track, where Vuetify drew `surface-variant` at 38% and the inactive track at 14%. An invalid slider keeps Vuetify's look. The defaults set only `color`, `trackColor` and `variant`, so an app's `color` prop still sets the active track and the handle, and `variant="default"` brings back Vuetify's round handle. material-web's browser test measures each part's colour and opacity, light and dark, and the track, handle and stop indicator.

- Fields open fast again: material-web's install script (`postinstall`) stops Vuetify's idle loaders reading layout as they mount ([vuetifyjs/vuetify#23393](https://github.com/vuetifyjs/vuetify/issues/23393)). Every `VField`-based input mounts an idle `VProgressLinear`, whose `useElementIntersection(root)` read `getBoundingClientRect()` at once, a forced layout per field in a form that writes a style as it mounts: 300 fields took 15.9 s to mount, 1.3 s patched (the package's browser test). The script passes `{ immediate: false }` to the three loaders' calls (`VProgressLinear`, `VProgressCircular`, labs' `VLoadingIndicator`) in the installed Vuetify's `lib/` modules and its `dist/vuetify(-labs).esm.js`, `.cjs` and `.js` builds, all or nothing per Vuetify copy, and only where it finds exactly the calls it expects; otherwise it prints one line naming the issue and changes nothing. It never fails an install. Approve it in the app's root `package.json` (`"allowScripts": { "@jskit-ai/material-web": true }`, which `jskit update` adds): a strict install (`strict-allow-scripts`) fails without the approval, and an install with `--ignore-scripts` leaves Vuetify as published until `npm rebuild @jskit-ai/material-web`. Remove it once a fixed Vuetify is pinned; the README's Upstream workarounds says how.
- `material.css` draws every `v-bottom-navigation` as Material 3's navigation bar: a destination (each of its stacked buttons) has its icon, its `prepend-icon` or the first icon in its content (bare or in a badge), 24dp in a 64x32 pill that is its active indicator (`secondary-container`, the icon `on-secondary-container`) and holds its hover, focus and pressed state layer, where Vuetify drew its overlay and ripple across the whole button; the label is label-medium, 4dp below, `on-surface-variant` at rest and `on-surface` when active. A comfortable bar (48dp) drops the 4dp and a compact one (40dp) has a 24dp pill, so neither clips a label. The colours apply only where the app set none: a bar's `bg-color` (other than a surface tone) or colour class, and a destination's `color`, `base-color` or colour class, keep the app's colour on label and icon, the active pill drawn in that colour. An append icon, a badge's own icon and `mode="horizontal"` keep Vuetify's look. It was drawn only by the shell's `ShellTabLinkItem`, so an app's own bar of `v-btn`s kept Vuetify's look.
- **Breaking (visual).** Fields (`v-text-field`, `v-textarea`, `v-select`, `v-autocomplete`, `v-combobox`, `v-file-input`) keep Vuetify's line for their supporting text, as a Material 3 text field does: the defaults no longer set `hideDetails: "auto"`. With it, a field had no line until it had a message, so an error shown on blur or submit pushed the rest of the form down under the person's finger, and forms made up the space with a margin under each field. Upgrading: in a form, remove the `mb-*` margin under each field (the line is the gap now); a field outside a form (a search box, a filter, a toolbar select, a composer) sets `hide-details`, or `hide-details="auto"` to show a message only when it has one.
- `material.css` has a `text-on-*` class for every `on-*` role of the scheme. It had them for the main roles, success, warning and info, but not for the fixed roles' (`on-primary-fixed`, `on-primary-fixed-variant` and the secondary and tertiary ones) or the surface tones' (`on-surface-dim`, `on-surface-bright`, `on-surface-container-lowest` to `-highest`), so `color="on-primary-fixed-variant"` found no class. Vuetify names an `on-*` role's class `.on-<role>` only; an app colour's `on-*` roles keep that class alone. material-web's node test checks the classes against the generated scheme's roles.
- An active list item's subtitle is at full strength too, in the item's own colour, as a selected Material 3 list item's supporting text takes its label's: it was at Vuetify's medium-emphasis opacity, 4.45:1 on a card's surface-container-low, under WCAG's 4.5:1. So is the subtitle of an item with its own text colour, in that colour; an item at rest without one is still on-surface-variant, and a disabled item keeps its fade. shell-web's adaptive-shell browser test measures both subtitles' contrast over the card and the active item's state layer, in light and dark.
- A text field's label and its supporting text (hints and messages) are on-surface-variant at full strength, as Material 3's text field draws them, instead of Vuetify's on-surface at the medium-emphasis opacity, which read 4.4:1 on a light surface, under WCAG's 4.5:1. A focused field's label keeps the field's colour, and an invalid field's label and messages keep error.
- A list item's subtitle is on-surface-variant at full strength, as Material 3's list draws its supporting text, instead of the item's on-surface at the medium-emphasis opacity (4.35:1 on surface-container-low). An active item, one with its own text colour and a disabled one keep theirs.
- Buttons' and pressable chips' state layers are their shape again: the 48dp touch target makes a button's overflow visible, which stopped it clipping Vuetify's overlay and underlay, so hover, focus and press drew a square on every round icon button (an app bar avatar, the rail's menu control) and on disabled ones. They now take the button's corners.
- A labelled switch sits after its label, at the end of the row, as Material 3 places it, instead of before it. A switch without a label of its own (one in a list row's append slot) stays where it is.
- `theme.typography: { brand, plain }` names Material 3's brand typeface (display, headline, title-large) and plain one (title-medium and -small, body, label) as CSS font-family values, both Roboto Flex by default. They become the theme variables `--v-font-heading` and `--v-font-body`, which Vuetify's type classes read; `material.css` keeps Roboto Flex for both only as a fallback in the `vuetify-core` layer, so an app's typefaces win. Upgrading: an app with its own fonts names them here instead of overriding `--v-font-body` or `font-family` in CSS.
- Cards have no default `elevation`: Vuetify's elevated variant (the default) keeps its level-1 shadow, and flat, tonal and outlined cards (banners, heroes, containers) have none, as Material 3 draws them; the same for cards in a bottom sheet.
- FABs keep Material 3's sizes instead of the common button's 40dp: 56dp, small 40dp, large 96dp (36dp icon), and the extended FAB 56dp tall.
- Small, x-small and large buttons get the 48dp touch target that default and icon buttons have, as a transparent hit area around the unchanged visual; stacked buttons are left as they are. The hit area is ink overflow (layout containment), so a button at the edge of a window or scroll container adds no scrollbar.
- A button label stays on one line and ends in an ellipsis when it is longer than its button, instead of spilling out of it.
- A theme colour can keep its own hue: `colors: { learning: { value: "#F5C518", blend: false } }` still gets its four roles, light and dark, without the shift toward the seed (Material Theme Builder's Blend switched off). A hex string, or `{ value }`, stays harmonized as before.
- `v-btn-toggle` is a Material 3 segmented button: a 40dp outlined pill (the outline role) whose segments share its width, each with a 48dp touch target; an unselected segment has no container, and the selected one is `secondary-container` and leads with a check, which takes the place of its icon. Its segments, and those of any `v-btn-group`, have 12dp sides instead of the common button's 24dp, so a group in a filter row's narrow `auto` column keeps whole labels instead of squeezing a segment to a sliver.
- Filter chips are Material 3's: chips in a `v-chip-group` (now `filter` by default) and chips that toggle with `aria-pressed` have an outline-role border with no container when unselected, and `secondary-container` with a leading check when selected; 32dp tall with 8dp corners, 16dp sides (8dp beside the check) and a 48dp touch target. Outlined chips draw the outline role, as every Material 3 chip does, instead of outline-variant.
- Fixed tabs (`v-tabs grow`) share the row's width equally without Vuetify's 90dp tab minimum, so five short ones fit a phone; when a label still can't fit, the tabs scroll instead of cutting it.
- A menu's own list is its container (surface-container at level 2, with the menu's 4dp corners), now drawn by `material.css`, and a list inside a card or sheet in the menu, a rich menu panel, is a plain list again. The defaults set nothing under `VMenu`, so a `v-select`'s typeahead scrolls to and focuses its match again, whether typed while the menu opens or after: under any `VMenu` sub-component default, Vuetify 4.2.1 to 4.3.2 rebuild the menu's root defaults when an ancestor of the select re-renders (a form does on every change), its virtual list resets its item height to 0, and the match further down ("o" then "s" for Osaka) was selected but never shown ([vuetifyjs/vuetify#23366](https://github.com/vuetifyjs/vuetify/issues/23366)). See the README's Upstream workarounds.
- The defaults set nothing under `VDialog`: material.css draws a dialog's card (surface-container-high at level 3, 28dp corners) and every card's 12dp corners, which the `VCard` default drew as an inline `rounded` style. Under any `VDialog` default, Vuetify 4.2.1 to 4.3.2 rebuild a dialog's defaults whenever an ancestor re-renders, as they do a menu's, so a virtual-scroll list in the dialog fed fresh items lost its item heights and `scrollToIndex` gave up. A bottom sheet's card is now the card's surface-container-low with square corners inside the sheet's 28dp top ones, as the `VBottomSheet` default meant but never reached (the dialog a bottom sheet renders starts again from the app's defaults); it was surface-container-high with rounded bottom corners. An app's `rounded-*` class on a card now takes effect, as its `rounded` prop always did.
- Dialog actions sit 24dp from the dialog's sides and bottom, 8dp apart.
- Cards rest flat: the default card is `variant="flat"` on surface-container-low, and `variant="elevated"` is Material 3's elevated card with its level-1 shadow. Expansion panels are flat by default. Dialogs keep their level 3.
- Tonal buttons and chips, disabled filled buttons, and every button's or pressable chip's hover, focus, pressed and selected layer are one pill again: their state layer, underlay and press ripple take the control's corners. Since 0.1.2's 48dp touch target unclipped them, Vuetify's square layers drew square tinted boxes behind the pill.
- A disabled filled, elevated or tonal button is Material 3's on-surface at 12% with a 38% label, whatever its colour, instead of a faded primary.
- A button with `to` is active only on exactly its route (`exact` by default), so a button to a section no longer shows selected on the pages under it; `:active="false"` on such buttons can go. Tabs keep their own.
- A pagination's current page has the activated state layer, so its number shows; it was a solid square, since Vuetify ties it to the border opacity.
- Adjacent buttons no longer take each other's presses. An icon button is never under Material 3's 32dp extra-small size, so `density="compact"` and `"comfortable"` icon buttons (20dp and 28dp since 0.1.2's 40dp icon buttons) hold their 24dp icon again; and a button that follows another one stops its 48dp touch target at its own edge on that side, so its target, painted over the earlier button, never covers it. A press or click on either button's visible box reaches that button, as in a table row's compact actions or a pair of tab actions.
- A shown placeholder is on-surface-variant at full strength (it was faded on-surface at 2.2:1); a labelled field still hides it until the field is active.

## 0.1.0

- New package: exact Material 3 for JSKIT Vuetify apps. `createMaterialVuetifyOptions({ seed, colors, icons })` generates the light and dark schemes from one seed with Material Color Utilities (the app's own `colors` as harmonized custom colours, light and dark), follows the system scheme, maps every icon alias to Material Symbols Rounded, and sets JSKIT's Material 3 component defaults; `client/material.css` adds the exact sizes and shapes (40dp buttons and 32dp chips inside 48dp touch targets, outline-variant dividers), Roboto Flex and motion.
